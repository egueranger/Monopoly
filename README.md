We provide a stochastic model to built Monopoly investing strategies.

monopoly.py runs a Monte-Carlo simulation of a real game with two dice and special squares on which one can be jailed or sent to some other squares with "Chance" and "Caisse de communauté" cards.

Bar chart monopoly.png displays the empirical frequencies for each square over the long run. The key idea is to estimate the invariant distribution since the process is Markovian. 
We can easily see a much higher frequency (60k VS 20k) in the jail (square 10). Otherwise highest frequencies are precisely those on cards (Boulevard de Belville, Boulevard de la Vilette, Boulevard Saint-Michel, Gare de Lyon, Avenue Henri Martin, Rue de la Paix) that give additional possibilities to reach them. From these squares with highest frequencies one can deduce that since the sum of two independent dice is more probable about 5-9 since it es not uniform between 2 an 12. Hence squares like Gare de Lyon, Boulevard Saint-Michel and Avenue Mozart are most of the time visited after exiting the jail and then are more often visited.

monopoly.xlsx displays all terrain and real estate costs, rents and estimated invariant distribution and computes return on capital (ROC) and furthermore the weighted-probability return on capital (WPROC). The last quantity we introduced is the expected return on capital with probabilities estimated by the Monte-Carlo simulation. For example, a 5 % ROC means that if you invest 100 F in the considered terrain you will get (surely) 5 F every time an adversary is coming to you, whereas a 5 % WPROC means that if you invest 100 F in the considered terrain you can expect (as probabilistic expectation) 5 F per adversary dice roll. All prices are in French Francs (currncy ticker FRF, short abreviation F).
Best ROCs are not necessary in the most expensive quarters, because terrains and construction costs are too high (30-40 kF for a single terrain, 20 kF for a house, 100 kF for an hostel). Best ROCs can be found in cheap quarters (6-12 kF for a terrain, 5 kF for a house, 25 kF for an hostel). Furthermore, terrains with best ROCs are not necessary best WPROCs if the probability to sleep there is too low. At the end of the day, best terrains regarding WPROC are the orange group : Boulevard Saint-Michel, Avenue Mozart and Place Pigalle, combining quite low construction costs and high rotation, they offer a WPROC almost twice higher than in eg. Avenue Foch.
